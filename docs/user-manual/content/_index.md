**Walldash** turns your house into a live 3D map of your smart home. Import your floor plan, place your devices, and control everything from a wall-mounted touchscreen or a tablet — no Home Assistant dashboard building, no YAML.

{{< figure src="/images/house-overview.png" alt="Walldash house overview showing stacked floors in 3D" caption="The **house overview** stacks every floor as an isometric model you can explore at a glance." >}}

## What you can do

- **See your home in 3D.** Each floor is rendered as an isometric model with walls, rooms and outdoor areas. Lights glow when they are on, and sensors float above the rooms they monitor.
- **Control devices with a tap.** Toggle lights, switches, plugs and appliances directly inside the 3D scene, or from a customizable dashboard.
- **Draw your plan.** Trace walls, create rooms and place doors and windows in the built-in 2D editor — or import an existing Sweet Home 3D file.
- **Monitor sensors in space.** Temperature and humidity readouts are projected onto each room, with comfort thresholds that pulse when a room gets too cold or too hot.
- **Build touch dashboards.** Compose grids of widgets — gauges, switches, sensor cards and favorite automations.
